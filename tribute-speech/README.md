# Tribute speech: Mrs. Teske

- `speech.md`: full speech in outline order (AG, credibility, purpose, preview, 3 main points, review, full circle, closure). About 555 words, which is about 4¼–4¾ minutes at a slow pace with clicks and pauses.
- `Tribute_Speech_Mrs_Teske.pptx`: 6 slides (Intro, Caring: Always There to Help, Caring: Made Class Fun, Dedicated, Ready for High School, Thank You), with gray placeholder boxes where the photos go. Each slide's speaker notes hold the part of the speech said on that slide.
- `notecards.md`: 5 notecards, using the same symbols and Spots A/B/C as the Bermuda cards.
- `build_slides.js`: source for the deck. Speaker notes are read from `speech.md`. Rebuild with `NODE_PATH=<folder with pptxgenjs + sharp> node build_slides.js`.

## Checklist against the criteria, planner, and notes sheet

| Requirement | Where it's met |
|---|---|
| 3–5 minutes | About 555 words, 4¼–4¾ min at a slow pace |
| AG: brief story / memory | Walking up to her desk and figuring it out the second you get there |
| Credibility: how you know them, what's unique | Her class at St. John's, about 10 students, she taught almost every subject for 2 years, sister had her too |
| Purpose: 1 sentence, relationship + full name, what they mean to you | "My 7th and 8th grade teacher, Mrs. Kata Teske, is one of the main reasons I was ready for high school." |
| Preview: 1 sentence, all 3 MP themes | Caring / dedicated / impact on the student I am today |
| Option 2: MP1 trait 1 + examples | Caring: same treatment as the only boy, slowing down when the class struggled, her desk, December treats, parties, stories |
| Option 2: MP2 trait 2 + examples | Dedicated: homework, no late work, hard tests, extra credit, hard work was normal |
| Option 2: MP3 impact of **both** traits | Caring → confidence; dedicated → ready for high school; closing line names both |
| Transitions between main points | After MP1 and MP2 |
| Review: 1 sentence, all 3 themes | Caring, dedication, impact on me |
| Full circle: revisit AG | Back to the walk up to her desk |
| Closure: positive thought about her + how the audience can use it | "She showed me that the teachers who expect the most… thank them. Mine was Mrs. Teske." |
| Focus on honoree, no embarrassing, no exaggerating | Every detail came from your answers; "it was not only her" keeps MP3 honest |
| Slides: min 3 content slides with text + pics, +1 for a 4 | 4 main-point slides (plus intro and conclusion), each with photos + a heading |
| At least 1 picture of her | Slides 1, 2, 5, and 6 |
| Words are headings/titles only | Only titles, short subtitles, and photo captions |
| Background color changed | Chalkboard green |
