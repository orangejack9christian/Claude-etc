# Tribute Speech Notecards: Mrs. Teske

5 cards, one per slide. Same system as the Bermuda cards.

**Key**
- `○ WORD` = circle the word
- `__word__` = highlight yellow
- `_word_` = stress that word
- Quotes = say those exact words
- `↓` = glance down · `↑` = look up
- `//` = short pause · `///` = longer pause
- ①–⑤ = card number, circled in the top-right corner

**Spots**
- Spot A: audience's right, away from the board.
- Spot B: center.
- Spot C: one or two steps toward the board, but never in front of it.

Card 1: Introduction

```
1  INTRO | SLIDE 1 | SPOT B                            ①
2  ↓  ↑ [SMILE]
3  ○ IMAGINE: stuck on a homework problem
4  walk up to the teacher's desk to ask for help
5  ↓ // second you get there → figure it out _on your own_
6  ↑ [SMILE] "In __Mrs. Teske's__ class, that happened all the time."
7  ///  ★ KEEP EYES UP
8  ↓ ○ CREDIBILITY: 7th + 8th grade / __St. John's Lutheran__, Buckley
9  about __10__ students / almost every subject
10 almost every school day for __2 years__
11 sister had her too / brother still goes there
12 ↓ ○ PURPOSE: "one of the main reasons I was ready for high school"
13 ↑ [3 FINGERS] caring / dedicated / impact on me
14 ///  [CLICK → SLIDE 2]
15 [MOVE → SPOT A / PLANT FEET]
```
