# Anatomy of Trackmania (2020): components, jobs and status as of October 2026

Research date: 2026-10-10. Items are 2026 unless labelled otherwise. Older material is labelled [historical] with its year.

## Studio: Nadeo size, location, ownership, teams and roles

### Takeaway
Ubisoft Nadeo is a small Paris studio. Ubisoft has published no headcount, and data-broker estimates put it at about 40 people. It was founded in 2000 and has belonged to Ubisoft since 2009. In Ubisoft's 2026 reorganisation, reports (based on leaked lists and German trade press) put it in "Creative House 4". No current job postings or official team chart were found, so the team structure has to be inferred from what the game ships and operates.

### Cited Findings
- **Location and founding.** Ubisoft's studio page says Ubisoft Nadeo is in Paris Montparnasse, France. It was founded in 2000 and acquired by Ubisoft in 2009. The page calls Trackmania "the cult multiplayer car racing game", names Shootmania, says its games run on PC, consoles and VR, and claims they bring together "tens of million players". The page gives no headcount, team structure or job openings, and links only to LinkedIn. — [Ubisoft Nadeo studio page](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/studio/nadeo)
- **Founders and early titles.** Nadeo was founded in 2000 in Paris by Florent Castelnérac and Pascal Hérold. Its first game was Virtual Skipper 2 (2002). Its games are TrackMania (2003), Sunrise (2005), Nations ESWC and United (2006), Nations/United Forever (2008), TM2 Canyon (2011), Valley/Stadium plus ShootMania Storm (2013), TrackMania Turbo (2016), TM2 Lagoon (2017) and Trackmania (2020). — [Wikipedia: Nadeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadeo)
- **Size at acquisition [historical, 2009].** GamesBeat reported that TrackMania had 10 employees. Ubisoft's CEO said the studio drew more than 700,000 monthly unique users with only ten full-time staff. Wikipedia gives a peak of over 700,000 unique monthly players and 10 million registered players by 2009. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/ubisoft-buys-nadeo-racing-game-studio-as-expansion-binge-continues/); [Wikipedia: Nadeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadeo)

  - LeadIQ gives an 11–50 band, with about 29 listed employees in France and a few in Poland and elsewhere. — [LeadIQ](https://leadiq.com/c/ubisoft-nadeo/5cb866d01d00006301d1be9e/employee-directory)

- **Creative Network.** About 20 other studios form a "Creative Network" that supplies technical support, QA and other development resources to the houses. — [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/ubisoft-creative-network/); [GamesWirtschaft](https://www.gameswirtschaft.de/wirtschaft/ubisoft-creative-houses-struktur-120226/)

### Inferences
- About 40 people is a plausible size given four quarterly campaigns a year, daily curation and esports operations. The small team leans heavily on community labour: mappers, the Trackmania Exchange (TMX) and Openplanet developers, and tournament organisers. Several jobs a larger studio would staff in-house, such as UGC hosting, modding tools and much of the esports circuit, are carried by the community.
- The functions the game needs, inferred from what it ships and operates rather than from any official chart:
  - Engine and physics programming
  - Network, online services and back end (leaderboards, matchmaking, clubs, Cup of the Day servers)
  - Editor and tools programming
  - Platform and console porting (PS, Xbox, Luna) and certification
  - Environment and block/item art (the 2026 "Vistas", 3,000+ blocks)
  - Vehicle art and handling (4 cars)
  - Skin and cosmetic art (Prestige skins)
  - UI/UX
  - Level design and campaign curation
  - Live ops and scheduling
  - Esports operations
  - Community management and moderation
  - Anti-cheat and integrity
  - Audio (composer and SFX)
  - QA
  - Marketing (partly at Ubisoft level)
  - Business, monetisation and store
- The September 2020 accusations concerned the studio head's management of artists' work, which suggests art and design were done in-house at the time.

## Engine and technology

### Takeaway
Trackmania runs on Nadeo's in-house, proprietary engine. Sources call it "GameBox" or the "ManiaPlanet" engine. It has a scripting layer (ManiaScript) and physics that players and reviewers describe as deterministic, which is what makes replays, "press forward" tracks and replay-input forensics possible. It launched on PC in 2020 and on PS4/PS5, Xbox One/Series and Amazon Luna in May 2023, with crossplay and cross-progression.

### Cited Findings
- **Engine name.** ModDB describes GameBox as Nadeo's proprietary engine, used in TrackMania, ShootMania and Virtual Skipper. Wikipedia lists TrackMania 2's engine as "ManiaPlanet", so naming differs between sources. — [ModDB: Nadeo](https://www.moddb.com/company/nadeo); [Wikipedia: TrackMania 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TrackMania_2)
- **ManiaScript [historical, ~2010].** ManiaScript was announced with TrackMania 2 as a scripting language that lets players add their own features to the game. — [Wikipedia: TrackMania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TrackMania); [GiantBomb](https://www.giantbomb.com/news/trackmania-2-announced-for-2010-release/1691/)
- **Deterministic physics.** A player review says "the physics are completely deterministic, so an exact sequence of inputs will always result in the exact same result". This is what enables scripted "Press Forward" tracks. The source is a review, not official documentation. — [Playday forum review](https://forum.playday.one/t/review-trackmania/16178)
- **Physics fixes [historical, 2022].** Nadeo shipped physics fixes after launch. The 1 Oct 2022 update fixed ice and water physics, including the "ice-wiggle" exploit. — [Wikipedia: Trackmania (2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trackmania_(2020_video_game))
- **Launch and console versions:**
  - Windows launched 1 July 2020; PS4, PS5, Xbox One and Xbox Series X/S launched 15 May 2023. The console build has its own track editor using larger block pieces. — [Wikipedia: Trackmania (2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trackmania_(2020_video_game))
  - Ubisoft's launch note says the console and cloud versions arrived on Xbox, PlayStation and Amazon Luna with crossplay and cross-progression across all platforms. The redesigned console editor added hundreds of blocks, taking the collection past 3,000 pieces. — [Ubisoft News, 15 May 2023](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-gb/article/5nuH3hEk11McSpf59WJsNY/trackmania-available-now-on-playstation-and-xbox-consoles); [Trackmania news 7039](https://www.trackmania.com/news/7039); [racinggames.gg](https://racinggames.gg/news/free-to-play-trackmania-arrives-on-consoles)
  - The September 2022 announcement also listed Stadia, which shut down before launch. — [TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2022/09/11/trackmania-is-coming-to-consoles-in-early-2023/)
- **Stores in October 2026.** The trackmania.com footer lists Ubisoft Store, Epic Games Store, Steam, PlayStation, Xbox and Luna. — [Trackmania news 9286](https://www.trackmania.com/news/9286)
- **Online systems [historical, 2020–21].** Early server instability and netcode problems were largely fixed through patches. — [Wikipedia: Trackmania (2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trackmania_(2020_video_game))
- **Paid access is per platform.** Access must be bought separately on each platform. — [Gamers Unchained](https://www.gamersunchained.com/news/trackmania-takes-away-free-content-to-be-more-realistic-and-compare-the-situation-with-the-efforts-of-the-studio)
- **Competition scripting.** Competitions can enforce Openplanet signature modes through ManiaScript, so ManiaScript is still the server and game-mode scripting layer in TM2020. — [Openplanet: signature modes](https://openplanet.dev/docs/tutorials/signature-modes)

### Inferences
- Because the physics are deterministic, a replay is essentially a file of inputs. That lets the game validate leaderboard replays and lets the community check them, and it also enabled the 2021 cheat detection.
- The proprietary engine means engine programming cannot be bought in through Unreal or Unity expertise. Engine, physics, network and editor work are core in-house specialties.

### Gaps
- No official technical documentation was found for the engine's language, server architecture or cloud hosting. That the engine is written in C++ and that Openplanet plugins use AngelScript is background knowledge not verified this session.
- No source was found on how Nadeo validates replays server-side.
- No source was found on whether Nadeo runs its own servers or uses Ubisoft-wide back-end services.

## Content: cars, environments, blocks, campaigns, Track of the Day and modes

### Takeaway
TM2020 has four cars (Stadium, Snow, Rally, Desert) and, since January 2026, four island "Vistas" alongside the classic Stadium. It has more than 3,000 blocks and items. A 25-track seasonal campaign rotates every quarter (Winter 2026 on 27 Jan, Summer on 1 July, Fall on 1 Oct 2026), each with 5 "Black" tracks that double as esports maps. Ubisoft Nadeo curates a daily Track of the Day from community maps, which feeds Cup of the Day. Other modes are Ranked (2v2 since July 2025), Royal, Stunt, Platform, Weekly Shorts, Weekly Grand Track/Enduro and Cup of the Week.

### Cited Findings
- **Cars (all dates from Wikipedia):**
  - Stadium: original environment, blocks rebuilt from scratch.
  - Snow car: 21 Nov 2023, with a wood surface.
  - Rally car: 27 Feb 2024.
  - Desert car: 22 May 2024; it tips onto two wheels in turns.
  - Source: [Wikipedia: Trackmania (2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trackmania_(2020_video_game))
- **Block and item drops [historical]:**
  - 12 June 2021: water, plastic blocks, and animated/dynamic items.
  - 14 Dec 2022: 290+ new blocks and items.
  - 29 Mar 2023: 150 new blocks and items.
  - Source: [Wikipedia: Trackmania (2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trackmania_(2020_video_game))
- **2026 Vistas.** The update of 27 Jan 2026 added four island Vistas: White Shore (Nordic), Green Coast (European valleys), Red Island (American canyons) and Blue Bay (Asian lagoons). The classic Stadium remains. Vista blocks are in the editor on PC and consoles, with special macroblocks on consoles. The Winter 2026 campaign's 25 tracks all use the Vistas, and its Black Tracks draw on China, France, the USA, Brazil and Greece. — [Trackmania blog: Update 2026](https://blog.trackmania.com/2026/01/26/trackmania-update-2026/)
- **Campaign cadence.** A 25-track seasonal campaign rotates every three months, alongside a permanent 25-track Training campaign. — [Wikipedia: Trackmania (2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trackmania_(2020_video_game))
- **Medal gating since Jan 2026.**
  - White and Green tracks are free.
  - Blue tracks need 10 bronze medals.
  - Red tracks need 10 silver medals.
  - Black tracks need 10 gold medals.
  - Source: [Trackmania blog: Update 2026](https://blog.trackmania.com/2026/01/26/trackmania-update-2026/)
- **2026 campaigns:**
  - Summer 2026 (1 July) had 5 Black Tracks themed on Argentina, Saudi Arabia, Poland, Norway and Japan. Their new 3D items went into the "Nadeo in-game club" for players to reuse. — [Trackmania blog: Summer 2026](https://blog.trackmania.com/2026/07/01/trackmania-summer-season-2026/)
  - Fall 2026 (1 Oct) has 25 tracks, with 5 Black Tracks themed on Egypt, the UK, the Czech Republic, India and New Zealand. Gold and Black medals are easier, and Author medals are harder on the first 20 tracks. The first 10 tracks are free and Club Access unlocks the rest. Many tracks are designed for Ranked. — [Trackmania news 9286](https://www.trackmania.com/news/9286); [simracing-pc.de](https://simracing-pc.de/en/2026/10/01/trackmania-fall-2026-author-times-author-medals/)
- **Black Track Identity Contest (Oct 2026).** Players are invited to create "identities" for 20 Black Tracks to be used in the 2027 seasonal campaigns, EWC and ENC. Creator submissions close 22 Oct, followed by a player showdown and vote on 27 Oct. — [Trackmania news 9286](https://www.trackmania.com/news/9286)
- **Who makes campaign tracks:**
  - At the February 2020 reveal, Ubisoft described the season as a regular official campaign plus a daily track chosen from player-submitted, peer-reviewed maps. — [Ubisoft News, 28 Feb 2020 (search snippet)](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/2TT6868Gzx9cZpbqOVXCJn/trackmania-nations-remake-revealed)
  - The 2026 official posts sampled (Update 2026, Summer, Fall) do not name track authors. — [Trackmania blog: Summer 2026](https://blog.trackmania.com/2026/07/01/trackmania-summer-season-2026/); [Trackmania news 9286](https://www.trackmania.com/news/9286)
- **Track of the Day:**
  - It is a daily showcase of community-created maps, curated by Ubisoft Nadeo, released at 19:00 CE(S)T. The page says Standard or Club Access is required; this is legacy wording, since Standard merged into Club in 2024.
  - Selection criteria: the route must be fully respawnable from every checkpoint, with no unnecessarily hard speed checks, clear signage, and be playable by beginner and intermediate players.
  - Players have 24 hours to set a time. The TOTD is used in the Cup of the Day knockout. Rewards run from 1 Trophy 7 (1,000,000 points) for 1st down to the top 100,000.
  - Source: [doc.trackmania.com: What is TOTD](https://doc.trackmania.com/play/what-is-totd/)
- **Map Review.** Players submit maps to Map Review servers, where other players rate them 1–5 stars. Per the docs, Ubisoft Nadeo's map selectors then use the ratings and review counts to decide whether a map qualifies for TOTD or Royal. A player blog claims, anecdotally, that a 4.2–4.3+ rating is needed to get a Nadeo review. — [doc.trackmania.com search snippet](https://doc.trackmania.com/create/map-review/play-others-maps/); [ReedyBear's blog](https://reedybear.bearblog.dev/trackmanias-weekly-shorts/)
- **Mode timeline:**
  - Cup of the Day: beta 2 Nov 2020, full release 12 Dec 2020, three times a day from 11 Aug 2021; requires Club.
  - Ranked (formerly Matchmaking): beta 4 Feb 2021, full release 1 June 2021, changed from 3v3 to 2v2 on 1 July 2025.
  - Royal and Super Royal: 12 June 2021.
  - Stunt: 1 July 2024. Platform: 1 Oct 2024.
  - Weekly Shorts: 17 Dec 2024, with a five-style format from 10 Mar 2025.
  - Cup of the Week and Elite Cup: 27 Jan 2026.
  - Source: [Wikipedia: Trackmania (2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trackmania_(2020_video_game))
- **New modes in 2026:**
  - Weekly Grand Track: a 10-lap race, Sundays 18:00 CET. Players start 32nd of 32 cars and must overtake 31. Starter Access.
  - Weekly Grand Enduro: 60 laps with medals every 5 laps, Sundays 16:00 CET. Free via the Arcade channel.
  - Cup of the Week: Saturdays 17:00 CET on Blue, Red and Black tracks; Club Access. The Black COTW runs 24 rounds eliminating 2 players each, then 15 rounds eliminating 1.
  - Source: [Trackmania blog: Update 2026](https://blog.trackmania.com/2026/01/26/trackmania-update-2026/)
- **Trophy economy.** A division-1 Cup of the Day win awards a Trophy 7 (1,000,000 points). First place on the official campaign's final ranking awards a Trophy 9 (100,000,000 points). — [Wikipedia: Trackmania (2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trackmania_(2020_video_game))

### Inferences
- Live-ops content volume is high relative to team size: 100 campaign tracks a year (4×25), 365 TOTD picks a year, weekly Shorts and Grand Tracks, and three Cups of the Day per day. Much of it rests on curating community maps rather than authoring from scratch. TOTD and Royal are selection jobs; campaigns and Black tracks need design, medal-time setting and art themes.
- Each season ships new art assets: 3D items for each country theme, Prestige skins and the Vistas. These are the main new-art workloads.
- Recurring tuning work includes setting medal times (Author, Gold and so on) and adjusting difficulty between seasons, as in the Fall 2026 changes.

Background knowledge suggests that since about 2021–2022 many official campaign tracks have been commissioned from credited community mappers, with Liquipedia keeping per-campaign author pages (e.g. [Summer 2021](https://liquipedia.net/trackmania/Nadeo/Campaign/2021/Summer)). This was not verified this session because Liquipedia returned HTTP 403.

- The track count for Summer 2026 was not stated in its post (25 is the standard).

## Editor, user-generated content, clubs, TMX, Openplanet and moderation

### Takeaway
User-generated content is central: a full block/item editor (simplified on consoles), custom items, skins, player-run Clubs, the fan-run Trackmania Exchange (TMX) for map hosting, and the fan-run Openplanet scripting framework, which Nadeo tolerates within a signed-plugin system. Moderation is mainly report-driven: in-game, on the website (with Report buttons on maps and clubs) or through Ubisoft support.

### Cited Findings
- **Editor.** The PC editor is block and item based. The console version (2023) has a redesigned editor with larger pieces and macroblocks, and the collection passed 3,000 blocks. Vista blocks are available on PC and consoles from January 2026. — [Wikipedia: Trackmania (2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trackmania_(2020_video_game)); [Ubisoft News, May 2023](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-gb/article/5nuH3hEk11McSpf59WJsNY/trackmania-available-now-on-playstation-and-xbox-consoles); [Trackmania blog: Update 2026](https://blog.trackmania.com/2026/01/26/trackmania-update-2026/)
- **Asset reuse.** Each season's Black Track 3D items are published to the "Nadeo in-game club" so creators can use them in their own maps. — [Trackmania blog: Summer 2026](https://blog.trackmania.com/2026/07/01/trackmania-summer-season-2026/); [Trackmania news 9286](https://www.trackmania.com/news/9286)
- **Paid features.** Club Access covers clubs, skin customisation, special campaigns, online rooms, training tracks and competitions. Accounts of the 2023 console launch disagree on whether the free Starter tier includes a limited editor or none. — [isthereanydeal](https://isthereanydeal.com/game/trackmania/info); [racinggames.gg](https://racinggames.gg/news/free-to-play-trackmania-arrives-on-consoles); [traxion.gg](https://traxion.gg/?p=77667)
- **Openplanet signature modes.** Openplanet, a fan-built scripting and plugin framework, has these modes:
  - Official: loads only bundled plugins.
  - Regular (default): also loads signed, approved user plugins.
  - School: practice plugins, with online play and official leaderboard submission blocked.
  - Developer: loads everything.
  - Writing plugins requires Club access.
  - Competitions can enforce a signature mode via ManiaScript.
  - Source: [Openplanet: signature modes](https://openplanet.dev/docs/tutorials/signature-modes); [Openplanet: writing plugins](https://openplanet.dev/docs/tutorials/writing-plugins)
- **School Mode (2024).** It arrived in Openplanet 1.27 because there had been no adequate way to stop Club players from running helper plugins (e.g. speed-drift helpers) in any context. A 2022 plan to allow visual speed-drift and ice-slide helpers was delayed "until further notice". — [Openplanet: Introducing School Mode (2024)](https://openplanet.dev/news/2024/introducing-school-mode); [Openplanet: visual SD helpers (2022)](https://openplanet.dev/news/2022/allowing-visual-sd-helpers)
- **TMX.** Trackmania Exchange is the community map repository. Third-party API libraries (e.g. py-trackmaniaio) expose TMX map records with upload and update timestamps, and hosting guides point server admins to TMX for custom maps. — [py-trackmaniaio TMX docs](https://py-trackmaniaio.readthedocs.io/en/latest/trackmania.tmx.html); [GameHostBros guide (Oct 2026)](https://www.gamehostbros.com/guides/games/trackmania/adding-custom-maps)
- **Moderation and reporting.** Players can report others in-game, on trackmania.com or through Ubisoft support. Maps and clubs have Report buttons on their web pages. Cases go to "the people in charge at Nadeo", reporters are not told the outcome, and players are told not to report via social media, DMs or Discord. — [doc.trackmania.com: how to report a player](https://doc.trackmania.com/general/how-to-report-player/)
- **Community map events (Fall 2026).** Kacky Reloaded 7 runs through November with 75 community-made maps revealed on 30 Oct. — [Trackmania news 9286](https://www.trackmania.com/news/9286)

### Inferences
- Nadeo outsources UGC hosting (TMX), modding and tooling (Openplanet) and much curation input (Map Review star ratings) to volunteers, and keeps the final selection and integrity gates in-house. Report review is a human moderation job at Nadeo, with no published sign of automated filtering.
- Openplanet's signing process means plugins are reviewed by Openplanet maintainers, not Nadeo. That is a community quality and integrity gate.

### Gaps
- No public figure was found for total TMX maps or total uploaded maps.
- No information was found on proactive or automated moderation of skins, map names or club content.
- The formal relationship between Nadeo and Openplanet or TMX (sponsorship, API agreements) was not found.

## Business model, pricing, player counts and revenue

### Takeaway
The game is free to play, with a paid Club Access subscription. Since 9 January 2024, Standard and Club Access have been merged into one Club Access tier at $19.99/€19.99 a year, and the free Starter tier gets only 10 of each season's 25 campaign tracks. Ubisoft publishes no player counts or revenue for Trackmania. Steam concurrency in 2026 averages about 1,400–1,500, a partial view since most players are likely on Ubisoft Connect, Epic or consoles.

### Cited Findings
- **Pricing history:**
  - At launch [historical, 2020], Starter was free, Standard was $9.99 a year, and Club was $29.99 a year or $59.99 for three years. — [Trackmania news 956](https://www.trackmania.com/news/956); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/trackmania-will-be-free-to-play-but-youll-need-to-subscribe-for-certain-features)
  - On 9 Jan 2024, Starter was cut from 25 to 10 campaign tracks per season, Standard was removed, and Club dropped from $29.99 to $19.99 a year. Ubisoft's FAQ says Standard and Club were merged into a new Club Access at €19.99/$19.99 a year, existing Standard holders keep access until expiry, and access is bought per platform. — [Wikipedia: Trackmania (2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trackmania_(2020_video_game)); [Gamers Unchained](https://www.gamersunchained.com/news/trackmania-takes-away-free-content-to-be-more-realistic-and-compare-the-situation-with-the-efforts-of-the-studio)
  - **Price conflict.** isthereanydeal, last updated February 2026, still lists Club at $29.99 a year or $59.99 for three years. Epic still sells a 3-year Club Access. — [isthereanydeal](https://isthereanydeal.com/game/trackmania/info); [Epic Games Store](https://store.epicgames.com/p/trackmania--club-access-three-years)
  - As of Fall 2026, the first 10 campaign tracks are free and Club Access unlocks the full campaign. — [Trackmania news 9286](https://www.trackmania.com/news/9286)
- **Reception.** The subscription model drew criticism, and the January 2024 Starter cut was especially unpopular. Metacritic 74/100; IGN 7/10, criticising the menus and subscription. — [Wikipedia: Trackmania (2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trackmania_(2020_video_game))

## Integrity: the 2021 cheating scandal, anti-cheat and leaderboard validation

### Takeaway
In May 2021, Wirtual and donadigo published a report showing that top players, notably riolu, had cheated world records for years by playing in slow motion. They found it by pulling inputs out of replays and scanning for implausibly twitchy steering, using a tool built by donadigo. Nadeo responded with investigations, leaderboard scans and an in-game reporting system. The fan-run Openplanet signing system and School Mode add a community-maintained integrity layer. No public 2025–2026 Nadeo announcement on anti-cheat or replay validation was found.

### Cited Findings
- **The 2021 report.** Wirtual and donadigo published it on 23 May 2021.
  - Method: donadigo built a tool to extract player inputs from replay files, plus a script that scanned TMX replays for twitchy steering.
  - Findings: many runs over more than nine years and across several titles were likely cheated, and six more players were accused. The authors admitted they did not investigate hardware or software latency effects, and some flags were false positives.
  - Before exposure, riolu held all 200 Trackmania Turbo world records and 62 of 65 TM2 Canyon world records. Riolu denied the accusations and then went silent; Techno and two others confessed to using Cheat Engine slow-motion.
  - Fallout: Nadeo started a cheating investigation across the franchise, and the fan-made "Competitive Patch" for older titles was adopted more widely.
  - Sources: [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/did-the-biggest-trackmania-streamer-get-caught-cheating); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/trackmania-cheating-scandal-is-utterly-fascinating-1846960428); [Wikipedia: Wirtual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wirtual); [tildes.net](https://tildes.net/~games/x1x/the_biggest_cheating_scandal_in_trackmania_history)
- **Follow-up [March 2025].** Wirtual's follow-up video alleged that riolu kept playing under aliases such as "92BOB" and "Eddy Reising". — [Wikipedia: Wirtual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wirtual)
- **TM2020 anti-cheat scan [historical, ~2020].** A player reported that an anti-cheat scan had wiped top runs, including world records, on campaign tracks as apparent false positives. Ubisoft forum staff (Ubi-Milky) asked for replays and screenshots, said times might not be restored, and said the aim was to find bugs and false positives in "the software Nadeo uses". — [devtrackers.gg](https://devtrackers.gg/trackmania/p/b1c5ba74-false-positive-runs-wiped-out)
- **Reporting system.** Cheat reports go in-game, on the website or through Ubisoft support to "the people in charge at Nadeo", and outcomes are not disclosed. — [doc.trackmania.com: how to report a player](https://doc.trackmania.com/general/how-to-report-player/)
- **Openplanet integrity layers.** Plugins are signed, School Mode blocks helper plugins from online and leaderboard contexts, and competitions can enforce signature modes via ManiaScript. — [Openplanet: signature modes](https://openplanet.dev/docs/tutorials/signature-modes); [Openplanet: School Mode (2024)](https://openplanet.dev/news/2024/introducing-school-mode)
- **Player criticism.** One Steam commenter argues the anti-cheat is easily broken and its methods "archaic and well known among cheat makers". This is an unverified player opinion. — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/232910/discussions/0/4691153621053044524)

### Inferences
- Integrity work is replay forensics: analysing input timing and steering patterns in deterministic replays. Much of the original detection capacity came from the community (donadigo, Wirtual). Nadeo's anti-cheat process is opaque.
- Leaderboard validation, plugin policy and tournament rules are recurring operational jobs, now raised in importance because $750K of 2026 EWC/ENC money depends on online qualifiers (COTWs).

### Gaps
- No official 2025–2026 Nadeo statement was found on anti-cheat technology, kernel anti-cheat, server-side replay validation or leaderboard audits. TMInterface (donadigo's tool-assisted tool) was not verified in this session.
- No information was found on Nadeo's staffing for integrity work.
